Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026  ·  Dave.AI

Who we are

Dave.AI ("Dave.AI", "we", "us", "our") operates the platform at daveai.law, which provides AI-assisted legal drafting and research tools for legal professionals.

For the purposes of UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, Dave.AI is the data controller for personal data collected through this platform.

Contact us: privacy@daveai.law

What data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data — your name, email address, and firm name provided at registration
  • Subscription data — your chosen plan, billing cycle, and payment status (we do not store card details — payments are handled by Stripe)
  • Usage data — features you use, documents you generate, and session activity logs
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device type, and cookies necessary for the platform to function
  • Content data — text you submit to our AI tools (contract instructions, document text, legal questions)

How we use your data

We use your personal data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Dave.AI platform
  • Manage your account and subscription
  • Process payments through our payment provider (Stripe)
  • Send you service-related communications (receipts, security alerts, product updates)
  • Comply with our legal obligations under UK law
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse of the platform

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell your data to third parties.

AI-generated content and your inputs

When you use our AI tools, the text you submit is transmitted to Anthropic PBC (our AI provider) to generate a response. This transmission is governed by Anthropic's API usage policy.

Important: Do not submit genuinely confidential client information, privileged communications, or sensitive personal data to our AI tools. Treat all AI-generated output as a starting point requiring professional review — it does not constitute legal advice.

We do not use your submitted content to train our AI models. Anthropic's data handling policies apply to API interactions; please review anthropic.com/privacy for details.

Legal basis for processing

Under UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases:

  • Contract performance — processing necessary to provide the service you have subscribed to
  • Legitimate interests — improving our platform, preventing fraud, and ensuring security
  • Legal obligation — complying with applicable UK law and regulation
  • Consent — where we ask for your permission for specific uses (e.g. marketing communications)

Data sharing and third parties

We share data with the following trusted third-party providers where necessary to operate the platform:

  • Supabase Inc. — authentication and database hosting (our database is in the EU, in Frankfurt)
  • Stripe Inc. — payment processing
  • Anthropic PBC — AI model inference (processing may take place outside the UK, including in the US)
  • Netlify Inc. — website and function hosting
  • Brave Software Inc. (US) — web search for the Research feature; receives the text of your research questions
  • EmailJS (US) — sends notification emails for sign-ups and contact/enterprise enquiry forms (name, email address, firm name and message); these emails are delivered to the founder's email inbox, hosted by Google
  • Google Fonts (US) — font delivery; receives visitors' IP addresses when pages load

All providers are contractually bound to handle data securely and in accordance with applicable data protection law. Where data is transferred outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place. Transfers to the US rely on the UK–US Data Bridge where the recipient is certified under it; otherwise we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses.

Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as we need it. Specifically:

  • Documents and research you save to History: on the Starter plan they are deleted automatically 30 days after they were created. On free trials and on Pro, Firm and Enterprise plans they are kept until you delete them or your account is closed. You can delete any saved item yourself at any time from History.
  • Text you type into the AI tools: we do not log it. It is processed to produce a response and kept only if you choose to save the result. Our systems record usage counts, timestamps and a one-way fingerprint of each request, which we use to apply plan limits and prevent abuse; they do not record the text itself.
  • Account data: kept for the duration of your subscription, plus up to 7 years for financial record-keeping where it relates to payments.
  • You may request deletion of your account at any time. See "Your rights" below.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies necessary to operate the platform (session authentication and security). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You cannot opt out of essential cookies without losing access to the platform.

We also use your browser's local storage for the following:

  • dave_session — caches your session and profile details so the platform works between pages (strictly necessary)
  • Supabase authentication token — keeps you signed in (strictly necessary)
  • dave_welcome — a temporary flag used to show a welcome message after you sign up, removed once shown (interface preference)

Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@daveai.law. We will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Security

We protect your data using 256-bit TLS encryption in transit and industry-standard security measures at rest. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel only. We will notify you and the ICO of any personal data breach in accordance with our obligations under UK GDPR.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or by a prominent notice on the platform. Continued use of Dave.AI after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
