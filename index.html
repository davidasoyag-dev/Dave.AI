FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before signing up.

Is Dave.AI accurate for UK law?
Yes. Dave.AI is trained and tuned specifically for England and Welsh law. Unlike generic AI tools, it references UK Acts, statutory instruments, and English case law rather than US or international sources. All output should still be reviewed by a qualified solicitor before use.
Will my client data be kept confidential?
Absolutely. Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We do not use your documents to train AI models, and we comply fully with UK GDPR. Your documents are yours.
How does the 14-day free trial work?
Sign up and get full access to every feature for 14 days — no credit card required. At the end of your trial, choose a plan and enter your payment details. If you don't upgrade, your account simply pauses. No surprise charges.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes, at any time. Upgrade or downgrade instantly from your billing settings. If you upgrade mid-month, we prorate the difference automatically.
How does Dave.AI compare to other legal AI platforms?
Enterprise legal AI platforms typically cost over £1,000 per user per month, with large minimum seat commitments that put the annual price well into six figures. Dave.AI starts at £49/month for a solo solicitor — making us 10x more accessible while delivering the same core AI power, built specifically for UK law. For larger firms needing 10+ users, we offer custom Enterprise pricing with dedicated support and SLA guarantees.
How does Enterprise pricing work?
Enterprise is for firms with 10 or more users who need a tailored solution. Pricing is agreed based on your team size, usage requirements, and any custom integrations or SLA guarantees you need. Contact us and we'll put together a proposal within 48 hours.
