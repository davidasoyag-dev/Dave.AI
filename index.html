FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before signing up.

Is Dave.AI accurate for UK law?
Dave.AI is built and configured specifically for the law of England and Wales. It's instructed to reference UK Acts, statutory instruments and English case law rather than US or international sources, and can draw on live search for recent changes. As with any AI tool, all output should be reviewed by a qualified solicitor before use.
Will my client data be kept confidential?
Yes. Your documents are encrypted in transit and at rest. To generate results, the text you submit is processed by our AI provider, Anthropic, who do not use it to train their models — and we never use your documents to train anything. We handle personal data in line with UK GDPR; see our Privacy Policy for the full detail, including a Data Processing Agreement for firms that need one.
How does the 14-day free trial work?
Sign up and get full access to every feature for 14 days — no credit card required. The trial includes up to 25 AI actions a day. At the end of your trial, choose a paid plan to continue (paid plans are launching soon; we'll contact you before any charge). If you don't upgrade, your account simply pauses. No surprise charges.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Once paid plans launch, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time.
How does Dave.AI compare to other legal AI platforms?
Enterprise legal AI platforms are typically built for large firms — high per-seat pricing and long commitments. Dave.AI starts at £49/month for a solo solicitor, is built specifically for the law of England and Wales, and has no lock-in. For larger firms needing 10+ users, we offer custom pricing — just get in touch and we'll put together a proposal.
How does Enterprise pricing work?
Enterprise is for firms with 10 or more users who need a tailored solution. Pricing is agreed based on your team size, usage requirements, and any custom integrations or SLA guarantees you need. Contact us and we'll put together a proposal within 2 working days.
